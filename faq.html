Applicant FAQs

Everything you might want to know before applying to Summer Camp '26. Can't find the answer? Email skylor@skylorlane.com.

  • Summer Camp '26 runs for the month of August, 2026. The program is 100% online, ending with an optional in-person demo day in Toronto.
  • Anyone, anywhere. Students, working professionals, career switchers, makers, dropouts, retirees — if you have an idea you're excited about and roughly ten hours a week to work on it, you're eligible.
  • No. Summer Camp is completely free. We don't take any equity or any ownership in what you create.
  • Yes — really. The program, the lectures, the community access, all of it. The only thing that costs money is travel + accommodations if you decide to come to the in-person demo day at the end. That's optional.
  • Travel grants are available on a case-by-case basis for accepted applicants who can't otherwise make it to demo day. Reach out after acceptance and we'll see what we can do.
  • Travel credit goes to applicants accepted into the cohort who complete a meaningful weekly update for at least four of the five weeks. We'll share details on how to claim it once the program starts.
  • Once accepted, you'll receive a welcome email with a link to your dashboard. Travel credit instructions live there. Don't worry about this until you're in.
  • We review applications on a rolling basis and send decisions within two to three weeks. If it's been longer, your application is likely in the final review pile — hang tight.
  • Email us at skylor@skylorlane.com with "visa" in the subject line and your full name, and we'll bump your application up the queue.
  • Part-time. We recommend at minimum ~10 hours a week. But the more time you spend, the better your project will be at the end.
  • Yes — all live talks and lectures are recorded, and the replays go up the same day. Watch on your own schedule.
  • Yes. Team up with whoever you want. There's no formal team registration — every camper still submits their own weekly update, but you're free to collaborate on the same project.
  • Demo day. The top projects get to present in front of investors, founders, and the rest of the cohort. The whole thing happens in Toronto, in-person, and yes — it's real demo day energy, not a recital.
  • Email skylor@skylorlane.com with "bug" in the subject. Include a screenshot, your browser/device, and the page you were on. We fix things fast.
