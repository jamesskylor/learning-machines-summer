/ Our Story

A summer for the people
who can't sit still.

Summer Camp is a free 5-week program for ambitious builders. We give you the structure, mentorship, peers, and momentum you need to ship something you're proud of — over a single summer.

It runs entirely online from August 1 to August 31, 2026, on nights and weekends, alongside thousands of others. There's no curriculum, no homework, and no instructor grading you. You pick the idea. We pick the cadence.

Why we exist

The most transformative thing that's ever happened to most of the people we admire was that they spent a single summer working obsessively on something. Y Combinator started this way. So did most of the people we'd quietly trade lives with.

The world is full of programs designed to teach you things. We're designing one to give you the cover, peer pressure, and community to actually ship.

What you'll get

  • A weekly rhythm: 1 lecture, 1 talk, 1 weekly update. That's it.
  • A community of thousands of builders working on their own thing in parallel.
  • Optional in-person demo day in Toronto at the end of August.
  • Lifetime access to the alumni network (and warm intros into it).

Who runs this

Summer Camp is independent and self-funded. No equity. No strings. No sponsor logos in your face. We exist because someone has to do this for the next generation of builders, and waiting around for someone else to is what got us here in the first place.
